## 3. Narcotics & NDPS Enforcement (Synthetic vs Cultivated)

Narcotics enforcement (NDPS Act) saw a major surge in 2026. In June 2026 alone, **1,232 NDPS cases** were registered.

```
                      NDPS DRUG SEIZURE COMPOSITION (JUNE 2026)
                      
   Cultivated / Processed (Cannabis/Ganja/Hashish)  =======> 53.0% (653 Cases)
   Synthetic / Chemical (MDMA/Meth/LSD/Cocaine)     =======> 47.0% (579 Cases)
```

- **Cultivated / Organic Drugs (653 Cases)**: Ganja cultivation in border forests (Western Ghats, North Karnataka border), commercial interstate smuggling from Andhra Pradesh / Odisha.
- **Synthetic Drugs (579 Cases)**: Concentrated heavily in Bengaluru City, Mangaluru City, and Manipal/Udupi educational hubs. Involves darkweb procurement, courier parcel interception, and foreign national peddler networks.

## 4. Crimes Against Women, Children & SC/ST Communities

Statutory protection legislation forms a critical component of state policing metrics.

### A. Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO Act) & Child Crimes
- **POCSO Cases (H1 2026 Total)**: **2,459 cases** registered (averaging ~410 cases/month; 374 cases in June 2026).
- **Kidnapping & Abduction of Children**: **2,070 cases** in H1 2026 (largely elopement of minors, with 3 cases registered for ransom).
- **Child Marriage Prohibition**: 86 cases registered in H1 2026.

### B. Crimes Against Women
- **Rape (Sec. 376 / Sec. 64 BNS)**: 63 cases in June 2026 (3 Gang Rape cases, 60 Other Rape cases). H1 2026 Total: **314 Rape cases**.
- **Cruelty by Husband & Relatives (Sec. 498A)**: 1,534 cases in H1 2026 (295 in June 2026).
- **Outraging Modesty (Molestation Sec. 354)**: 3,303 cases in H1 2026 (502 in June 2026).
- **Dowry Deaths**: 69 total cases in H1 2026 (68 by non-burning means, 1 by burning).

### C. SC / ST Atrocities Act & Civil Rights
- **SC/ST Prevention of Atrocities Act**: **1,184 cases** registered in H1 2026 (217 cases in June 2026).
- **Highest Incidence Regions**: Kalaburagi, Vijayapur, Belagavi District, Tumakuru, Kolar, and Chitradurga.
