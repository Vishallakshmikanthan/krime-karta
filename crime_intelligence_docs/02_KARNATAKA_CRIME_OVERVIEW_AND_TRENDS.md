## 1. Executive Summary: Statewide Crime Trajectory

Across Karnataka, crime monitoring is broadly bifurcated by statutory classification:
1. **IPC / BNS Crimes**: Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (formerly Indian Penal Code) offenses comprising conventional body, property, statutory, and personal crimes (Murder, Dacoity, Robbery, Burglary, Theft, Hurt, Rape, Kidnapping, Cheating).
2. **SLL Crimes**: Special & Local Laws offenses comprising state-specific and special central enactments (Karnataka Police Act, NDPS Act, POCSO Act, Arms Act, Gambling, MMDR/KMMCR, Excise, SC/ST POA Act).

## 5. Strategic Observations on Trend Shifts

1. **Murder Rates Peak in Summer Months**: Murder cases climbed from a low of 76 in February 2026 to a peak of 113 in June 2026 (+48.6% increase). Property disputes, personal vendettas, and water/land conflicts escalate sharply during heat waves and pre-monsoon agricultural prep.
2. **Cyber Crime Stabilization at High Volume**: Cyber crime averages ~950 cases per month. While lower than 2024 monthly spikes (1,600+), cyber financial frauds represent over 80% of economic crime losses in urban centers.
3. **Massive Surge in Narcotics Enforcement (NDPS)**: June 2026 witnessed a massive enforcement spike with **1,232 NDPS cases** registered compared to 814 in May (+51.3% jump), reflecting state-wide anti-drug drives targeting synthetic drugs (MDMA, Methamphetamine) and cannabis cultivation.
4. **Proactive Preventive Action Scaling**: Law enforcement increased BNSS preventive security proceedings against repeat offenders and rowdies, averaging ~4,900 preventive cases per month to curb violent crime ahead of regional festivities.
